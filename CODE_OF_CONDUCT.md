# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's information.
- Accept feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and help repair harm.
- Keep technical discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation, flag the content to a maintainer. For a report that
requires confidentiality, use the private contact method published on the
[@rsocko GitHub profile](https://github.com/rsocko). If no private contact
method is available, open a minimal public issue requesting a private channel
without naming people or describing the incident. Do not include unnecessary
personal data.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy as far as
practical, and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban a participant. Retaliation against a reporter
or witness is prohibited.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project.
