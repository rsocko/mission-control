Quick task update — concept comparison

Capture what happened without losing what happens next.

The core job is simpler than a full activity system: append a timestamped update quickly, then offer only the obvious next-step changes detected in the sentence.

Recommended direction Lean+: keep Notes uncluttered until needed. A Quick update button opens a small anchored composer that appends the entry and offers due-date or subtask suggestions.

Why this is the best fit

SpeedHigh · one line
Strengths
  • Preset verbs remove typing while natural language handles exceptions.
  • The preview makes AI parsing observable and reversible.
  • History and future action remain separate concepts.
Important rule

Do not silently mutate the task. Suggested effects start checked, remain editable, and show which changes will sync upstream.

Mobile

Expose “Update” in the fixed task action bar. Open a bottom sheet with the same verbs, one input, and vertically stacked effects.

Why Lean+ is enough

Capture timeA few seconds
Pros
  • No persistent controls competing with the Notes content.
  • The complete update stays readable in the source notes.
  • Date and follow-up inference remove the repetitive task edits.
Cons
  • Updates are not independently searchable or reportable.
  • Appending must guard against concurrent note edits.
  • Long-running tasks may eventually accumulate busy notes.
Verdict

Start here. Add structured activity only after real usage proves that filtering or reporting these updates is valuable.
