Common filters stay one click away

Level, State, Source, and Time remain in the familiar dashboard-style rail.

Advanced filters scale

Category, Merchant, Repository, Owner, Reason, Subject type, Source account, and Participating use one searchable builder.

Saved views remain prominent

Built-in and custom views live at the top of the rail; saving appears only for a new filter combination.

Actions are separated

Global actions stay in the title row and bulk actions appear only after selection.

Alternative considered: preserve the full facet rail and only move the rare free-text criteria behind “More filters.”
